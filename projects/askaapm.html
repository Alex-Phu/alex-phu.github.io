AskAAPM

First author · submitted to AAPM 2027 Spring Clinical Conference and JACMP

A retrieval-augmented system that answers questions about AAPM Task Group Reports and cites the source passages. Submitted to the AAPM 2027 Spring Clinical Conference and the Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics.

What I built

  • A question-answering system for AAPM Task Group Reports.
  • Every answer cites the source passages it was based on.

How it works

  1. 1Task Group report PDFs are ingested
  2. 2Reports are split into passages that can be searched
  3. 3A question pulls back the most relevant passages
  4. 4A short answer is written from those passages
  5. 5The answer is shown with quoted evidence and citations

Tools

  • RAG
  • FastAPI
  • Streamlit
  • FastMCP

Status

  • Submitted to the AAPM 2027 Spring Clinical Conference.
  • Submitted to the Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics.
