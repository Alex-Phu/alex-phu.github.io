ChessAI Assistant

Custom YOLO models and OpenCV read the board from Ray-Ban Meta camera feeds. Stockfish then suggests moves and explains tactics as the game unfolds.

What I built

  • Custom YOLO models that find each piece and label it with its color, type, and square, such as bR @ a8 for the black rook on a8.
  • Board reading with OpenCV that turns the detected pieces into a full position, written as a FEN string.
  • Stockfish analysis that suggests the next move for the side to play and explains tactics as the game unfolds.
  • Two ways in: a camera pointed at a physical board (including Ray-Ban Meta camera feeds), or Chess.com through screen capture.

How it works

  1. 1Frames come in from a camera or the screen
  2. 2YOLO finds and labels each piece
  3. 3OpenCV maps pieces to squares
  4. 4The position is written as FEN
  5. 5Stockfish suggests the next move

Tools

  • YOLO
  • OpenCV
  • Python
  • Stockfish
